How Mantis Works

Three steps from murky waters to crystal-clear visibility

01

Cast the Net

Drop in your domains, IPs, or ASNs and Mantis trawls the depths — discovering subdomains, mapping infrastructure, and surfacing assets you didn't know were exposed.

02

Crack the Shell

Every discovered asset gets probed, fingerprinted, and scanned for vulnerabilities. Mantis peels back the layers — open ports, exposed services, misconfigurations, the whole reef.

03

Stay on the Hook

Continuous monitoring keeps watch over your attack surface like a mantis shrimp stalking its prey. Pipe findings straight into Splunk, Sentinel, or your SOAR platform — or get alerts via Slack and email. Nothing slips past the reef patrol.

How Highlander Works

Three steps to smoke out the imposters in your waters

01

Bait the Trap

Drop in your domain and Highlander's proprietary engine dredges up thousands of potentially impersonating domains — typosquats, homoglyphs, and lookalikes lurking in the deep.

02

Inspect the Catch

Each suspicious domain gets hauled up and examined — active infrastructure, visual impersonation scoring, and threat analysis. The bottom feeders have nowhere to hide.

03

Clean the Reef

Review your catch in the dashboard, prioritize the biggest threats, and take action — takedown requests, defensive registrations, or just keep watching them squirm on the hook.
