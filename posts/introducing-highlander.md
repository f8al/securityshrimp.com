Domain typosquatting remains one of the most effective techniques attackers use to phish users and impersonate brands. A single mistyped character can send your customers to a convincing clone of your website, ready to harvest credentials.

That's why we built **Highlander**.

## What is Highlander?

Highlander is a domain typosquatting detection and brand protection platform. It uses our proprietary discovery engine to identify thousands of potentially impersonating domains and checks which ones are actually registered and potentially dangerous.

## Key Features

- **Proprietary discovery engine** — uncovers impersonating domains across multiple threat categories
- **DNS intelligence** — gathers comprehensive DNS data for discovered domains
- **Visual impersonation detection** — automatically identifies domains that visually mimic your brand
- **Threat scoring** — prioritizes discovered domains by risk level
- **Scheduled scans** — set up daily, weekly, or monthly automated monitoring
- **Real-time progress** — watch scan results stream in as they're discovered

## How It Works

Enter a target domain and Highlander goes to work, generating and checking domain permutations across multiple threat vectors. Each discovered domain is analyzed for active infrastructure and assessed for visual similarity to your legitimate site.

High-risk domains are flagged and scored, making it easy for brand protection teams to identify and take down threats quickly.

## Getting Started

Highlander is open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/securityshrimp/highlander). It runs as a containerized application with Docker Compose, making deployment straightforward.
